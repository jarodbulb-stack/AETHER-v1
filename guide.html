Quick Start
1
Load Sample Data from Command Center first. It fills every page with a working demo campaign so you can see how everything behaves before touching your real life with it — and clearing it later never affects anything real.
2
When you're ready, either Clear Demo and create your first real mission yourself, or use "Set Up AETHER v1.0" in Command Center to auto-create one honest first mission — literally about building AETHER itself, with real steps already marked verified.
3
Create a Blueprint for that mission — this is where the actual checklist lives. A mission with no blueprint has nothing to check off, so progress stays at 0%.
4
Check off steps as you genuinely complete them, and log Evidence against the ones that matter. Watch Life Advancement — that domain's route actually draws itself in as you go.
5
Set a real deadline on missions that matter. AETHER will keep reminding you — on Command Deck, in its own voice, and again and again if it's missed — until it's actually done.
